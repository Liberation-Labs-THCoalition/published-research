## Key Finding
A "deception direction" extracted via contrastive activation extraction from deceptive-pressure frames is, in this model, a composite: output-consequentiality substrate (L23-L31) + pressure-specific component (L35-L47). Five-stage Agni-gated confound elimination. Two depth profiles separating three pressure conditions (threat, social, reward). No deceptive behaviour observed (Test B: 0 of 21 scored threat trials inflated; shakedown: 0 of 3, 1 of 3 and 0 of 1 scored), so every claim concerns how the model represents deceptive-pressure framing, not deceptive behaviour.
