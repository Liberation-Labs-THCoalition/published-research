Abstract
Single-turn KV-cache keys carry the emotion of the text being processed: prior work, corrected in October 2026, decodes from keys the emotion a generator was asked to write a
story in, at 12.3× chance. That decode is not signicantly above a text (TF-IDF) baseline on the same stories (0.410 against 0.370; Holm-corrected p = 0.071), and its margin depends on a residualization tted on each test fold (0.348, exploratory, when tted on the training fold). The method that work describes is not above text (TF-IDF): 0.358 against 0.382, at 10 folds. We ask whether an emotion signal in the keys accumulates across conversation
turnswhether the model builds up a reading of the conversation's emotional tone that grows with interaction (the tone of scripted messages, not a user's state). Four experiments give a partial answer: the pooled cheerful/hostile gap shows no monotone trend with dose
(0.53, 0.72, 0.53 and 0.78 at doses 1, 2, 4 and 8), per topic it grows in one, shrinks in another
and changes sign in the third, and three topics cannot test either; these data do not settle whether emotion in the keys is stateless.
A dose-response experiment (18 emotional turns) shows a cheerful/hostile gap with no
monotone trend in dose (raw gap ∼0.50.8, read over equal-width chunks of the sequence
that mix user, assistant and probe tokens, not over the user's own tokens) while the readings
themselves drift with dose and sequence position (r= − 0.89 to −0.93). Each cell's n=9 is
three topics times three reruns, so an exact test with topic as the unit cannot go below
p=0.25. An emotional dynamics experiment reported a contrast overshoot at a tone shift
and a residual uncertainty scar after a neutral gap. Both are withdrawn: at the measured turn the two arms being compared were sent dierent user messages, so a dierence in their readings is what the instrument would show with no eect of history (Section 3).
System-prompt persona descriptions shift the probe encoding along one axis, read by
two directions built from overlapping emotions (valence dav= − 0.68; the anxious-minuscalm direction the code calls uncertainty, +0.73), consistent across all ve probes, though n=5 pairs cannot reach p<0.05 by permutation and the script behind this experiment is not shipped. Whether emotional context can be stated rather than experienced is not settled
by it. We oer a weather/climate framework as a hypothesis only: the readings have no meaningful zero, and whether accumulated history reaches distant token positions is not separated from sequence position.

5 Discussion
5.1 Weather, Not Climate
We had read the compass as reading emotional weatherthe current turn's emotional content but that is not established: its readings have no meaningful zero and drift with sequence position (Section 2), Experiment 1's read site mixes user, assistant and probe tokens, and its readings have never been checked against text. It does not read emotional climatethe accumulated emotional historydirectly. The climate was read as inuencing the weather through three mechanisms, none of them now established:

8

1. Contrast dynamics (withdrawn). The overshoot compared dierent messages at the
measured turn (Section 3).
2. Residual traces (withdrawn). So did the uncertainty scar.
3. Dose-dependent leakage (not established). The dose-8 probe-chunk gap (p=0.056,
uncorrected) is read in a chunk that also holds the end of the preceding emotional response, so it does not show leakage to distant positions. As a hypothesis, not a nding: the system prompt may act as a climate setting that shifts the compass reading on neutral probes, with conversation history as weather within it. Experiment 3 cannot establish the rst part (Section 4).
5.2 Implications for the Oracle Loop
These experiments support no recommendation about where to read tone for real-time alignment monitoring: Experiment 1 never isolated the user's own tokens (Section 2), and Experiment 2 read an assistant-header token. (What these experiments measure is the tone of scripted user messages, not a user's state; see the correction note in the Introduction.) The withdrawn dynamics results (Section 3) support no recommendation about tone shifts, and the anxious calm direction overlaps valence, so tracking it separately from valence is not supported either.
5.3 Architectural Interpretation
The KV cache stores per-position keys that encode each position's local context [Vaswani et al., 2017]. Attention mechanisms aggregate across positions, allowing the model's hidden state to
reect accumulated history. The WK compass reads keys, not the attention-weighted aggrega-
tion. At an identical probe its readings vary with the preceding history and with sequence length (Section 2), so these data do not show that a key reects only what was processed at its own position. Its local readings have not themselves been checked against text: the cited single-turn numbers describe other probes (see the correction note), and the companion entity result may reect familiarity rather than epistemic state.
Neither experiment uses that locality as designed. Experiment 1 averages equal-width chunks that mix user, assistant and probe tokens, and Experiment 2's read site is an assistant-header token that is identical in every arm, so everything it reads there comes in from the rest of the context.

7 Conclusion
The transformer's emotional context system is more nuanced than either accumulates or stateless would suggest, but less is established than the rst version of this paper said. The pooled gap between scripted cheerful and hostile conversations shows no monotone trend as their dose
grows (0.53, 0.72, 0.53 and 0.78 at doses 1, 2, 4 and 8; per topic it grows in one, shrinks in
another and changes sign in the third), read over chunks that mix user, assistant and probe tokens, on three topics. The contrast and scar results are withdrawn: each compared dierent messages at the measured turn (Section 3). Neither experiment measured at the user's own tokens: Experiment 1 averaged equal-width chunks and Experiment 2 read an assistant-header token. The compass's readings have not been checked against text.
Whether the compass registers changes of weather, or follows the climate at all, is not established by these data. A re-run with turn boundaries found by the tokenizer, identical measured-turn text across arms and several independent scripts would test it.
