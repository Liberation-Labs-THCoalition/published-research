### Anchor discipline — one near-miss worth recording

The first mine5 patch used two long multi-line anchors that happened to span the
abliteration clause. It matched 5/5 on the short anchors and **0/5 on two long ones in
three files**, because that clause diverges between editions. The script's rule
(*any site short of exactly-1 → leave the whole file untouched*) fired correctly: two files
were patched, three were skipped, and I reverted the two from backup and re-anchored below
the divergence rather than loosening the pattern. Had the rule been "patch what matches",
two copies would silently have diverged further from the other three — the T1b defect,
manufactured by the tool meant to close it.
